Services offered

Focusing on NetSuite and ERP:

  • Training and guidance at all levels, whether you're new to NetSuite or an experienced administrator wanting to deepen your skills.
  • Troubleshooting to help you figure out why something's not working — from saved search formulas to simplifying complex workflows — and explain it clearly.
  • Code review, especially if you're building custom SuiteFlow and SuiteScript and want to ensure it's efficient, maintainable, and follows best practices. An hour of my time now can save you days of debugging later.
  • Advice on whether NetSuite is the right fit for your business needs — sometimes it is, often it's not, and honesty saves money.
  • Ongoing NetSuite support — if you need a reliable expert to manage your system and deliver consistent results, I can support you across a wide range of requirements.

Focusing on finance transformation:

  • Process optimisation — analysing your current Order to Cash, Procure to Pay, and Record to Report cycles, with detailed recommendations on how to make them faster and more reliable.
  • M&A integration planning — helping you onboard newly acquired businesses into your NetSuite environment cleanly, without disrupting operations.
  • System architecture review — examining your entire finance applications ecosystem and suggesting improvements for data flow, automation, and reporting.
